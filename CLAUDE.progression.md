# Progression

> Part of the split `CLAUDE.md` docs — see [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md) for git/versioning workflow and cross-cutting gotchas.

- Promotion tiers at levels 1, 15, 40, 70, 100, 110, 120 — confirmed.
- Currencies: Gold, **Comet** (was "Meteor"), **Fallen Star** (was "DragonBall") — genuinely renamed everywhere (DB columns, RPCs, client identifiers) via migrations from `20260803090000_rename_meteor_dragonball_to_comet_fallen_star.sql` onward. Comet = Level Upgrade currency only. Fallen Star = Quality Upgrade currency **and** weapon-socket currency. Earlier doc history used "Meteor"/"DragonBall" — not retroactively rewritten in old notes, but all current code/schema uses Comet/Fallen Star.
- **Pet drop rate**: `PET_DROP_CHANCE` = 1/25,000 per kill. Live pet-obtained toast (`usePetToastStore`/`PetToast.tsx`, 4s auto-dismiss). `OfflineProgressModal.tsx` shows an amber "NEW PET" card and a violet "RARE DROP" card for a Fallen Star found while away.
- **EXP curve**: `MAX_CHARACTER_LEVEL = 130`. `EXP_CURVE_ANCHORS`/`requiredExpForLevel` in `useProgressionStore.ts` hold real Conquer-sourced anchors at levels 1, 20, 21, 80, 81, 109, 110, 127, 128, 130 (~13.4B total EXP to 130), geometrically (log-scale) interpolated between them. **Known imprecision**: summing 1-129 gives ~16.4B, ~23% over the cited total — widest gap is 110→127 (spans the 120 promotion boundary), likely not smooth exponential in the real game; revisit with more anchors if they surface. `addRewards` stops accumulating EXP entirely once `level >= 130` (gold unaffected). `ExpBar.tsx` shows "MAX" when capped.
- **"Ascend"** — confirmed design, **NOT implemented**. At level 130, Ascend resets level to **15** (not 1), reduces every item to its level-15-or-nearest-below template within its family chain while **preserving** quality tier, composition level, sockets, and every other bonus. **Capped at 2 Ascensions per character.** `getAttributesForLevel`'s curve has no ascension-count awareness — open question for whenever this is built. Do not build any part of this without further discussion.
- **Comet/Fallen Star kill-drop rates**: flat, deliberately unscaled by monster level — **1/500** per kill for Comet, **1/20,000** for Fallen Star (`COMET_DROP_CHANCE`/`FALLEN_STAR_DROP_CHANCE`, formerly `METEOR_DROP_CHANCE`/`DRAGONBALL_DROP_CHANCE`, in `combatResolver.ts`'s `rollBonusCurrencyDrops`), independent per-kill rolls, unaffected by rare status. Shared by live combat and offline simulator. Granted via atomic writes (see below) rather than a naive read-then-write, to avoid a lost-update race with concurrent Forge spending.
  - **Atomicity**: `resolve_combat_apply_rewards(character_id, gold_delta, exp, level, comet_delta, fallen_star_delta, comet_scroll_delta default 0, resolved_at default now())` (migration `20260805000000_add_resolve_combat_atomic_rewards.sql`, extended with the `comet_scroll_delta` param + return column in `20260808000000_achievement_comet_scroll_rewards.sql`, `service_role`-only) does the final write as one atomic `column = column + delta` SQL statement, returning real post-write values which `resolve-combat/index.ts` uses in its response instead of a stale JS-computed blanket update. Scoped to `gold`/`comet_count`/`fallen_star_count`/`comet_scroll_count` only — `exp`/`level` stay plain absolute overwrites (safe since `resolve-combat` is the sole server-side writer of those two fields). Adding the new param required dropping the old 7-arg signature first (`create or replace` alone would have created an ambiguous overload — the same `draw_lucky_ticket` gotcha noted elsewhere in this file).
  - **Meteor/Comet Scroll**: 10 loose units bundle into 1 Scroll (`comet_scroll_count`/`fallen_star_scroll_count`). `bundle_currency_scroll(character_id, currency_type)` (fixed 10-in), `unbundle_currency_scroll(character_id, currency_type)` (destroys 1 Scroll → 10 loose, all-or-nothing, requires 10 free Inventory slots computed server-side via the full occupied-slot formula — **excluding equipped and Bank-Storage gear** from the gear count, a bug that recurred in both this RPC and `resolve-combat`'s own room-check and was fixed in both). Both the Bundle action (Comet/Fallen Star tiles) and the Open/unbundle action (Comet Scroll/Fallen Star Scroll tiles, moved out of a below-grid card 2026-08-07) live in a click-opened `TooltipActionPopover` on the tile itself.
  - **Scroll Bank/Bank All (2026-08-08)**: Bank-tab-only, alongside the existing Open action — banks a Scroll straight to its full 10-unit value via the *same* `transfer_currency` RPC the loose-unit Bank/Bank All buttons already call (`InventoryPanel.tsx`'s `handleBankScroll`/`handleBankAllScroll`, amount `10`/`scrollCount * 10`), **not** a two-step unbundle-then-bank. Works because `transfer_currency`'s deposit direction already auto-unbundles exactly enough Scrolls to cover a shortfall in loose units (see the smart-deposit note below) — passing a Scroll-sized amount never touches Inventory room at all, unlike Open/unbundle which does.
  - **Smart currency auto-unbundling**: `ensure_loose_currency(character_id, currency_type, amount_needed)` (internal helper, no `authenticated` grant — only called by other `SECURITY DEFINER` functions) is called by `quality_upgrade`/`level_upgrade`/`master_forge_upgrade`/`unlock_weapon_socket` before their cost check — auto-unbundles exactly enough Scrolls to cover a shortfall, leaving remainder as loose units, refusing the whole transaction (`'not_enough_room_to_unbundle'`) if there's no Inventory room, before deducting any cost — no partial completion. All four callers' jsonb responses include the post-unbundle Scroll count (`fallen_star_scrolls_remaining`/`comet_scrolls_remaining`/Master Forge's generic `scrolls_remaining`, migration `20260808020000_forge_functions_return_scroll_counts.sql`) — added 2026-08-07 after a real bug (reported by the user): the response only ever carried the loose-unit count, so a Scroll consumed via auto-unbundle left the client's local Scroll count stale (too high, a "ghost" tile) until an unrelated full reload.
  - **Room-check formula fix (2026-08-07, migration `20260808030000_fix_ensure_loose_currency_room_check.sql`)**: the original room check compared `occupied + scrolls_needed*10` against the 40-slot cap — the full intermediate unbundle amount, as if it would sit in Inventory permanently. It never does: every caller immediately spends the full `amount_needed` right after this returns, so the true final state only keeps the small leftover change (`loose + scrolls_needed*10 - amount_needed`, always 0-9) as new loose tiles, while `scrolls_needed` Scroll tiles are freed. Correct check is `occupied + (9*scrolls_needed - amount_needed) > 40` — reported by the user: paying an exact 28-Comet cost with 2 Scrolls + 8 loose was wrongly refused, even though that transaction nets *10 fewer* occupied slots (2 Scroll tiles + 8 loose → 0, since the whole 28 is spent). Strictly less restrictive than the old formula, never more.
  - **Client-side affordability previews must count Scrolls too**: `effectiveCurrencyAvailable(looseUnits, scrollCount)` (`forgeCosts.ts`) — `MasterForgePanel.tsx`'s `blockedReason`, `ForgeSocketsPanel.tsx`'s Unlock Socket button, and `ForgePanel.tsx`'s quality/level disabled-reason text all use it now. Before this fix (2026-08-07, reported by the user: "it's not picking up that I have 4 Comet Scrolls... it seems it can only see the 6 individual comets"), `MasterForgePanel`'s Confirm button was hard-gated on loose units alone (`owned < cost`), blocking a genuinely affordable Master Forge attempt before the server-side auto-unbundle ever got a chance to run. `ForgePanel.tsx`'s regular Quality/Level Upgrade Confirm button was never actually gated on affordability at all (`previewItem` doesn't check funds) — so it was unaffected in practice, but its disabled-reason text was fixed for accuracy anyway.
  - **Forge cost model**: flat **1 Comet** / **1 Fallen Star** per attempt (Level/Quality Upgrade respectively), spent regardless of outcome. No qualifying unit on hand (`comet_count`/`fallen_star_count` < 1, after auto-unbundle) means the upgrade can't be attempted — no auto-withdraw from Bank.
  - **Gear Bank — two independent deposit paths**: "Deposit as Item" (`deposit_item`/`warehouse_items` legacy fungible-token system) is **removed entirely** (table + functions dropped, migration `20260803080000_bank_account_wide.sql` era) — superseded by real physical Bank Storage (below). "Deposit as Composition" destroys the item, adds only its `composition_level`'s point value into a pool **scoped to `slot_type`** (`players.gear_composition_points` jsonb, six pools — weapon/ring/necklace/boots/hat/coat, not cross-fungible). Depositing a Normal (composition_level 0) item is rejected (`no_points_contributed`). `withdraw_gear_composition(character_id, template_id, composition_level)` lets the player pick any class-available template of the matching slot_type + a tier to pay for.
- **Bank / Storage — fully account-wide, unified under one tab labeled "Bank" (internal `TabId`/files still say `warehouse`/`Warehouse*`/`useWarehouseStore` — display-text rename only, not a code rename, except where noted).**
  - Physical item storage (`item_instances.location: 'inventory' | 'bank'`) is real, non-fungible, full-identity storage — a Bank Storage slot behaves exactly like an Inventory slot (quality/level/composition/sockets all preserved). `deposit_item_to_storage`/`withdraw_item_from_storage(item_id, p_character_id)` are `SECURITY DEFINER` RPCs — withdrawal re-points `owner_id` to whichever of the account's 5 characters withdraws (not necessarily the depositor), since Storage is account-wide. Capped at 40 slots (`WAREHOUSE_SLOT_CAP`/`BANK_SLOT_CAP` in `useWarehouseStore.ts`/`useBankStore.ts`, mirroring `INVENTORY_SLOT_CAP`, client-side only).
  - Comets/Fallen Stars still have "store as a physical unit" (`comet_bank_count`/`fallen_star_bank_count`) via `bank_currency_item(character_id, ..., direction, amount)`, vs. "convert to points" (`transfer_currency`→account-wide `bank_gold`/`bank_comets`/`bank_fallen_stars`) — but **Deposit into the physical path was retired from the UI 2026-08-07** (confirmed with the user): only `withdraw` survives (`useBankStore.withdrawCurrencyItem`, still reachable from `BankGrid.tsx`'s Storage-grid tiles), so any already-banked physical units stay reachable but the balance can no longer grow. Composition Stones' equivalent physical path (`bank_stone_item`, `composition_stones_banked` jsonb) was retired the same day in both directions — the SQL function and column still exist (untouched, not migrated away, in case of already-banked balances) but nothing in the client calls them anymore; stones move through the points system (`transfer_stone`→`bank_points`) exclusively now.
  - Gold/Comets/Fallen Stars are account-wide, shared across all characters — not slot-based, just per-character/bank number pairs (`transfer_currency`, same backend for all three).
  - **UI**: two toggle buttons (character's own name / "Account") switch a single main area between Inventory grid and Storage grid (`WarehousePanel.tsx`'s `view` state) — no drag-and-drop between them anymore (click-based popovers only). Per-item popover on `InventoryPanel`'s `enableBankDeposit` prop (Bank-tab-only): gear keeps **Deposit**/**Deposit All** (physical storage) plus **Bank**/**Bank All** (liquidate to composition points, hidden for `composition_level === 0`); Comet/Fallen Star tiles instead show **Bundle** (10→1 Scroll) + **Bank**/**Bank All** (2026-08-07, replaces the old Deposit/Deposit All/Bank/Bank All 4-button set); Composition Stone tiles show **Bank**/**Bank All** only. `BankSquares.tsx` — a grid of simple label+quantity squares, always visible regardless of toggle: 3 currency squares (Gold/Comets/Fallen Stars Bank balances, with real icon art for Comets/Fallen Stars + quantity below, `grid-cols-3`), 1 "Composition Points" square, 1 "Gear Points" square (no number of its own — a category button, not a balance). Simplified 2026-08-07 (confirmed with the user) from the earlier 9 individual stone-tier squares + 6 individual gear-slot-type squares: clicking **Composition Points** opens a slider (`TierSlider` in `BankSquares.tsx`, tiers 1-9) to pick which stone tier to withdraw, capped by affordability (`compositionPointValue(tier)`); clicking **Gear Points** first opens a slot-type picker (weapon/ring/necklace/boots/hat/coat, each showing its own points total), then the same slider (0-9, 0 = free Normal item) plus a template `<select>` to choose which item to withdraw. Gold keeps Deposit+Withdraw (no Inventory tile of its own, so this square is its only interaction).
  - **Security**: all mutating RPCs are `SECURITY DEFINER`, ownership-verified, one transaction. `item_instances`' existing owner-account-scoped SELECT already permits reading any of the account's characters' items — only the client query needed widening (`useWarehouseStore.loadBankItems(accountId)`, kept separate from character-scoped `useInventoryStore.items`).
  - **Gotcha, recurring pattern**: a room/occupancy check that counts `item_instances` for a character must exclude `location = 'bank'` rows and every one of the 7 `equipped_*_id` slots — this exact miscounting bug hit `unbundle_currency_scroll`, `resolve-combat`'s own gear-count room check, and needs re-verifying in any future room-check code.
- **VIP Token (groundwork only, v1.107.0)**: a consumable that grants VIP status — no gameplay bonuses implemented yet, this is plumbing only. `characters.vip_token_count` (virtual-tile counter, same shape as Comet Box — one non-stacking Inventory tile per owned token, no `item_instances` row) and `characters.vip_expires_at` (nullable timestamptz, null = never VIP). `use_vip_token(p_character_id)` consumes 1 token and sets `vip_expires_at = greatest(coalesce(vip_expires_at, now()), now()) + interval '30 days'` — stacks on top of remaining time rather than replacing it. Client: `useBankStore.useVipToken` (cross-store mutation: `useCurrencyStore.vipTokens` + `useCharacterStore.vipExpiresAt`), `VipStatusHud.tsx` shows "👑 VIP · Xd left" in the top HUD strip whenever `vipExpiresAt` is in the future, and a 👑 badge renders next to a sender's name in Global Chat (`chat_messages.is_vip`, a send-time snapshot written by `send_chat_message` — not a live lookup, same "snapshot at write" convention as `seller_character_name`).
  - **Acquisition**: Lucky Lad only (weight 0.02 in `pick_lucky_reward`'s Hyper Rare bucket — rarer than every other reward, including the three existing hyper-rare gear kinds at 0.06-0.08), funded by trimming Class 1 Money Bag 11.8→11.78. Also grantable via Admin Mail (`mail.currency_type = 'vip_token'`). Not in `draw_lucky_ticket`/`draw_lucky_ticket_bulk`'s `v_needs_room` list — a counter grant is never room-gated, same precedent as Comet Box.
  - **Not yet built**: Bank/Marketplace listing support for the token itself (not a `ListableCurrencyType`, mirrors Lottery Ticket/Ascension Points).
  - **VIP Automation menu (v1.108.0-1.108.11)**: the first real VIP perk — opened via `VipStatusHud`'s badge, `VipSettingsModal.tsx`. Four independent toggles, persisted through `set_vip_automation_settings` (`characters.vip_automation_settings` jsonb, outside the authenticated column-allowlist so it needs its own `SECURITY DEFINER` writer): Auto-Sell Ore, **Auto-Sell Normal Gear** (Normal-quality gear only, gold sale — its only automatable fate, since Salvage gives 0 AP for Normal and it usually has no Composition level to Bank; `'normal'` was briefly a selectable `autoSalvage` minTier in v1.108.10 before being reverted here in favor of this dedicated toggle, since salvaging a Normal item nets nothing), Auto-Salvage Quality Gear (minimum tier `tempered`/`infused`/`radiant`/`ascended`, default `tempered`), Auto-Bank +N Gear (minimum Composition level 1-12). A `bank_first`/`salvage_first` priority setting resolves items that qualify for both Salvage and Bank; Auto-Sell Normal Gear only fires when neither of those two claimed the item (Bank still wins if a Normal item happens to have `composition_level > 0`). All four just replay the existing manual `sell_item`/`salvage_item`/`deposit_item_as_composition` RPCs — no new mutation logic of its own. Driven by `runVipAutomationPass()` (`src/game/vip/runVipAutomationPass.ts`), the single entry point called both reactively during live play (`VipAutomationEngine.tsx`, 800ms debounce) and synchronously right after offline/AFK catch-up (`offlineProgress.ts`/`offlineMiningProgress.ts`), so `OfflineProgressModal`'s VIP-summary section has results ready before it renders. Sweeps **both** real Inventory items and Loot Holding entries (offline catch-up routes drops into Loot Holding, not Inventory — missing the Loot Holding half was a real bug, fixed in `481eac6`); Normal gear in Loot Holding sells directly via `sell_loot_holding`, same as Ore, without claiming into Inventory first. Gated to only touch items that actually came from a combat/mining drop (`isDropSourced()`, `src/game/items/dropSourceTracking.ts`) — a Bank withdrawal or Mail claim landing in Inventory must never get swept up (fixed in `94c6583`, after a bug where a Bank-withdrawn item got auto-salvaged within the same second). Locked items (Equipment tab lock) are always excluded from every rule. `VipAutomationSummary`'s gold field is split `oreGoldGained`/`gearGoldGained` so the welcome-back modal's two Auto-Selling rows don't double-attribute each other's gold.
- **Meteor/DragonBall (Comet/Fallen Star) icon art**: `public/item-icons/meteor.png` (blue/purple comet — used for Comet), `public/item-icons/dragonball.png`→now referenced as `fallen-star.png` (old art temporarily reused, real Fallen Star art still blocked on the user). `InventorySlot`'s `iconSrc` prop (image, priority over emoji `icon`) is the shared mechanism — `METEOR_ICON_SRC`/`DRAGONBALL_ICON_SRC` constants in `forgeCosts.ts`. Own border/glow colors via `InventorySlot`/`qualityColor`: `DRAGONBALL_COLOR` (Fallen Star) its own orange; `MATERIAL_COLOR` (old Infused-Silver `#C8D0DC`) shared by Comet + Composition Stones + future Gems; `CONSUMABLE_COLOR` shared by Potions (green).
