# Combat & Loot

> Part of the split `CLAUDE.md` docs — see [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md) for git/versioning workflow and cross-cutting gotchas, including the `resolve-combat` duplication warning this file's math depends on.

## Core loop

**Melvor Idle-style menu-driven idle RPG**, superseding an earlier isometric-canvas action-combat build (Phaser, tile movement, manual attack-range/positioning). Melvor is the reference for *how* the game is presented (menu-driven, auto-idle combat, tabbed pages); Conquer Online remains the reference for *what* the game contains (classes, gear, gems, zone/monster names, etc.).

Combat is fully automatic: pick a zone, pick one monster from that zone's roster, hit Fight, and it resolves via a tick-gated resolver (`src/game/combat/`) — no movement, no manual attack timing, no positioning. A 2-hour-capped (scales with Prestige/account tier — see `CLAUDE.progression.md`) offline/idle-progress simulation (`src/game/combat/offlineProgress.ts`) reuses the same resolver logic to calculate rewards for whatever monster was last selected while away, applying rewards through the same code paths a live kill uses, with a summary modal (`OfflineProgressModal.tsx`) on return.

## Combat

- **Implemented**: `src/game/combat/combatResolver.ts` (pure rare-roll/HP/reward math), `useCombatStore.ts` (live tick-gated resolver), `CombatEngine.tsx` (non-visual, mounted unconditionally in `GameShell`, drives `runTick` off a 100ms `setInterval`). `runTick` gates each attack on `1000/attackSpeed`, requires Quiver for Hunter, resolves damage via `resolvePhysicalDamage`.
- Gold/EXP/item-drop grants happen instantly on kill. **Monster respawn gap (2026-08-17, requested by the user, replaces the original instant-respawn design; raised 2s → 10s in the 2026-11 rebalance below)**: a fixed `RESPAWN_GAP_MS` (10s) now sits between a kill and the next monster appearing — mirrored in `useCombatStore.ts` (client, `respawnReadyAt` field — while waiting, `currentHp`/`maxHp` are 0, `runTick` skips both the player's attack and the monster's attack-back, same "neither side acts" shape the knockout timer uses; `CombatPage.tsx` shows a `RespawnBanner` in place of the portrait/HP bar) and `resolve-combat/index.ts` (server). The old instant-respawn model let a fast, overleveled character's attacks look like they landed more than once per attack-interval tick once a monster's remaining HP dropped low relative to hit damage — the gap fixes the visual pacing and also now caps real kill throughput regardless of character power (a one-shot kill still can't happen faster than once per `RESPAWN_GAP_MS`).
- **Weapon curve / enemy HP / composition rebalance (2026-11, requested by the user)**: characters were trivializing content 3-5 levels above them — even modest gear investment (Tempered quality, +3 composition, one gem) 2-4 shot a same-level-bracket monster at every level from 20 to 130, because monster HP (`maxHp ≈ 8× a same-level Bow's damage`, the old formula) was blind to quality tier/composition/gem stacking, and Bow's own attack curve **tapered** at high level (54% growth per 10 levels at Lv20-30, only 14% by Lv120-130) so a stale weapon cost *less* DPS the further into the game you got. Fixed with two paired changes, which must move together:
  - **Bow's `physical_attack` curve reshaped into smooth compounding growth** (~2.577%/level, pinned at the unchanged level-8 (7) and level-130 (156) values) instead of the old front-loaded/tapering shape — makes a stale weapon cost a consistent, larger % of damage at every level (was ~13% for a 20-level-old weapon at level 130, now ~26%), not just early on. `dexterity` re-derived per the existing `round(atk × 0.15)` rule. Ring reshaped the same way off its own natural rate (~3.128%/level, pinned at level-1=2/level-126=94). Club/Longsword/Blade/Backsword re-derived off the new Bow curve using the *same* preserved ratios from `20260913000000_rescale_weapon_curves.sql` (club ×1.2224, longsword ×0.8679, blade ×1.0066, backsword ×0.3213 on `magic_attack`) — migration `20261101000000_recompound_weapon_curves.sql`.
  - **Every monster's `maxHp` re-derived from scratch** against the new curve (not a flat multiplier) — target: Infused quality, level-matched gear, +0 composition, no gems ≈ 6 hits vs. a same-level monster (the user's stated 4-8 target). Validated: Normal-quality lands ~9 hits, Ascended ~5, and full realistic BiS (Ascended, +12 composition, both weapon sockets on Ascended Drake gems) lands flat at ~3 hits across the whole 1-130 range (the accepted ceiling — the user wanted "1-2" but even maxed-everything landed at 2-3, and that was accepted rather than stacking further multipliers to force it lower). `goldReward`/`attackDamage` untouched. Migration `20261102000000_rebalance_enemy_hp.sql`, mirrors into `zoneData.ts`'s `EnemyTypeDef.maxHp` client-side.
  - **`COMPOSITION_BONUS_PCT_PER_TIER` raised 5% → 10%** (`equipmentBonus.ts`) — at 5% even max composition (+12) barely moved a fully-invested character's hit count. Both **item_templates stats are read live** (not baked into `item_instances` at drop time), so this whole rebalance retroactively changes every already-owned character's gear/every monster instantly on deploy, not just future drops/spawns — confirmed with the user.
  - **Damage-dealt EXP removed** (was `DAMAGE_EXP_SHARE = 0.5`, a flat +50% EXP bonus on top of the full on-kill grant, credited proportionally to damage dealt even on non-killing hits — a kill used to net 150% of `expRewardForLevel`). Requested by the user specifically because of the `RESPAWN_GAP_MS` increase to 10s — EXP is now on-kill only. The underlying reward math is otherwise **unchanged** — still the same 2026-08-11 closed-form expected-value engine (`expectedKillsThisWindow`/`creditedKills` → `killExp`), not a revert to per-attack RNG simulation; only the extra damage-based term was dropped. Removed from `combatResolver.ts`'s `expectedRewardPerAttack` (and its now-dead `RARE_BLENDED_DAMAGE_EXP_FACTOR` constant), `expCurve.ts`'s `DAMAGE_EXP_SHARE` export, and `resolve-combat/index.ts`'s mirror (also dropped the now-unused `totalExpectedDamage`/`creditedDamage` locals it fed).
  - **Server reward math — cycle-time model, not a discrete-event walk**: unlike Row Combat (multiple simultaneous/switching targets, needed a full event walk), a single fixed target with a fixed gap reduces to simple closed-form math: `dps = hitChance × expectedDamagePerHit / attackIntervalMs`, `timeToKillMs = effectiveHp / dps`, `cycleTimeMs = timeToKillMs + RESPAWN_GAP_MS`, `expectedKillsThisWindow = elapsedMs / cycleTimeMs`. Replaces the old `totalAttacks × hitChance × expectedDamagePerHit` formula and its overkill cap entirely — kills are now bounded by wall-clock cycle time rather than raw damage output divided by HP, so the old `maxUsefulDamage` cap (and the bug it fixed, a level-100+ character idling a level-1 monster racking up 12,493 "kills") can't recur under this model. `totalExpectedDamage` (feeds `DAMAGE_EXP_SHARE`) is derived separately from the same cycle time, crediting only the post-gap "active" portion of any partial cycle. No new persisted state needed — purely a function of `elapsedMs`, same as before.
- **Rare monsters**: 5% chance/kill (`RARE_CHANCE`), 2× HP (`RARE_HP_MULTIPLIER`), 5× gold/EXP (`RARE_REWARD_MULTIPLIER`), all in `combatResolver.ts` (shared with offline simulator so odds never drift). Visual: pulsing glow (`.super-quality-glow`-style) on monster portrait/HP bar.
- **Damage formula**: `resolvePhysicalDamage`/`monsterDefense` — `attack - defense`, floored at 10% of `attack` (`MIN_DAMAGE_PERCENT_OF_ATTACK`). `attack = physicalAttack + magicAttack`, summed uniformly for every class (not branched) — this is what makes Wuxia (Spirit-based, physicalAttack always 0) deal real damage at all today. Player's own Defense is modeled: monster-attack-back uses `resolvePhysicalDamage(monsterAttackDamage(type), effectivePlayerDefense)`.
- **Active skill system (2026-10, first entry)**: `src/game/skills/skillData.ts` (`SkillId`/`SkillDefinition`/`SKILL_TYPES`/`SKILLS_BY_CLASS`) + `useSkillsStore.ts` (equip/unequip, hydrated/saved via `characters.equipped_skill_id` — plain client-writable column, same session/cosmetic trust tier as `selected_monster_id`, column-level grant added in `20261018000000_add_equipped_skill.sql`). An equipped skill **replaces** the character's regular auto-attack (not an on-use ability like Multi-Shot below) — its own `attackIntervalMs` overrides `derived.attackSpeed`, and damage switches to `magicAttack`-only (`derived.magicAttack` + the skill's own flat `effectDamage`, Ember-gem-boosted like any other magic subtotal) instead of the uniform physical+magic sum. **Deliberately does NOT implement the other half** of the design this supersedes ("no skill equipped → `physicalAttack` only") — a skill-less Wuxia still gets the uniform sum, unchanged, since branching that too would nerf existing characters and wasn't asked for. Both `equippedSkillId`/`character.equipped_skill_id` are re-validated (class + `characterLevel >= requiredLevel` match) at the point of use, in both `useCombatStore.runTick` (client, predictive) and `resolve-combat/index.ts`'s own `SKILL_TYPES` mirror (server, authoritative) — never trusted at face value, matching every other client-writable combat-affecting column in this project.
  - **First skill**: `thunder` (Wuxia only), sourced from Classic Conquer Wiki's Taoist "Thunder" spell, Magic Level 0 row only (Cost 1 MP, Hit Rate 100%, Char Level 1) — per-level scaling (Magic Level 1-4) is explicitly deferred, every skill is its level-0 row for now. `attackIntervalMs: 1000` (no sourced cast-time data — same baseline as the regular 1.0/s auto-attack). **`effectDamage` recalibrated 7 → 1 (2026-08-27)**: the wiki's own value 2-hit-killed Windhollow's level-1 Quailwing (24 HP) for a level-1 Wuxia on the starter Lucky Backsword, reported as far too strong — this game's monster-HP curve isn't a 1:1 match for the source game's, so the flat value needed recalibrating rather than copying verbatim (same precedent as dodge chance/quality drop rates elsewhere in this file). Now a reliable 3-hit kill in that matchup.
  - **MP is a real, drained resource now** — first consumer of `derivedStats.ts`'s pre-existing `mp` field. `useCombatStore.currentPlayerMp`/`maxPlayerMp` mirror `currentPlayerHp`/`maxPlayerHp`'s exact shape (lazy-init from `derived.mp`, continuous across respawns/zone switches, never reset by `start()`/`stop()`/`clear()`). Spent on the cast attempt itself (hit or miss alike), never on a landed-hit-only basis. Insufficient MP blocks the attack outright (`'no-mana'` log kind, mirrors `'no-quiver'`'s "blocked attempt still advances `lastAttackAt`" shape) — no fallback to a plain physical attack, and no passive regen (matches this game's existing "HP never regens on its own either" precedent).
  - **No MP bar existed anywhere in the combat UI (bug, fixed 2026-11, reported by the user)** — MP was real and spent since the skill system shipped, but a Wuxia player had no visual warning before Thunder started silently failing on `'no-mana'`. `CombatPage.tsx` now renders an `HpBar` (reused, `bg-sky-500`) for `currentPlayerMp`/`maxPlayerMp` right below the HP bar, plus a Mana-potion quick-use row mirroring the existing HP-potion one — both gated on a local `activeSkill` re-derivation (class + level re-validated, mirrors `useCombatStore.runTick`'s own) so classes with nothing that spends MP don't show an irrelevant bar. Desktop and mobile layout blocks both updated.
  - **MP gating now simulated server-side too (bug, fixed 2026-11, reported by the user — "I think my character has run out of mana and isn't attacking" but was still gaining EXP)**: `resolve-combat` used to always assume the skill fired every `attackIntervalMs` regardless of MP — a real, live-play-visible bug (not just an offline edge case, since `resolve-combat` reconciles every ~`RESOLVE_INTERVAL_MS` during a fight), silently over-crediting kills/gold/EXP for the entire time a Wuxia was actually sitting idle out of mana. Fixed with a new persisted `characters.current_mp numeric` column (null = never tracked yet / presumed full, same lazy-init sentinel shape as the client's own `currentPlayerMp`). `resolve-combat/index.ts` now: reads it, caps `totalAttacks` (and the reward math's effective elapsed time) to however many casts the starting MP can actually afford, and persists the resulting new total back through `resolve_combat_apply_results`' new `p_current_mp` param (migration `20261105000000_server_side_mp_gating.sql`, which needed an explicit `drop function` first per the signature-change gotcha). `use_potion_stack` now also credits `current_mp` on an MP-potion use via a hand-mirrored case statement of `potionTypes.ts`'s MP-tier heal amounts (the SQL side has no equivalent of the client's attribute-interpolation table to compute a true max-MP cap, so this doesn't clamp to it — `resolve-combat`'s own read clamps to the real max on every resolve instead, self-correcting any minor overshoot; a potion used while already at the null/"presumed full" sentinel is correctly a no-op). Server's own `SKILL_TYPES` mirror gained the `mpCost` field it was previously missing entirely.
  - **Mana potions are no longer inert** (see `CLAUDE.inventory-and-equipment.md`) — Use now calls `useCombatStore.restorePlayerMp`, same clamp-to-max shape as `healPlayerHp`.
  - **UI**: `SkillsPanel.tsx`, a new card on the Equipment tab (`EquipmentTabPage.tsx`, below `StatsPanel`) with a Skills/Passives sub-tab switcher (`.btn-gold`/`.btn-gold-active`, same in-page-sub-nav convention as `CombatPage`'s Hunting/Mining/Events). Passives has no content yet — a stub tab. Equip/Unequip buttons per skill, level-gated.
  - **Not extended to World Boss/Row Combat** — both keep their own independent attack-math duplication untouched; a Wuxia with Thunder equipped still uses the old uniform sum there. Known, accepted scope gap for this first pass.
  - **Dodge/hit-chance**: `rollIsHit(dodge)` — incoming evasion, PLACEHOLDER `DODGE_CHANCE_PER_POINT` capped at 50% (`MAX_DODGE_CHANCE`). `rollAttackLands(playerDexterity, monsterDodge)` — outgoing hit chance, mirrors `rollIsHit`, sharing the same per-point/cap constants. **Dodge and Dexterity are separate derived stats**: `dodge = Agility×1 + Boots' own dodge base_stats key` (feeds `rollIsHit`, incoming only). `dexterity = Agility×1 + Bows'/Rings' own dexterity base_stats key` (feeds `rollAttackLands`, outgoing only). `monsterDodge(type) = Math.round(level * 0.8)` (formula-derived, not stored, unsourced placeholder pacing). Both incoming (client-only, `useCombatStore.runTick`) and outgoing (mirrored in `resolve-combat/index.ts`, since it affects real kills) paths wired in. **`DODGE_CHANCE_PER_POINT` lowered 2026-08-11 from 0.005 (0.5%/point) to 0.0015 (0.15%/point)**, confirmed with the user as "way too high" — Hunter is the Agility specialist and its Agility alone crosses 100 by around level 45 (`classes.ts`'s `ATTRIBUTE_ANCHORS`), which saturated the 50% cap on its own from that level on, before any Boots/composition dodge bonus. The new rate means only a true best-in-slot level-130 Hunter (Agility 275 + Ascended Boots' dodge, ~341 points total) approaches the 50% cap. Since the constant is shared with `rollAttackLands`, this also makes the player's own outgoing attacks whiff more often on a monster whose Dodge outpaces their Dexterity — a deliberate, symmetric change, not incoming-only. A **"Miss" floating combat text** (added same day) now shows for both directions — `CombatLogKind`'s existing `'miss'`/`'dodge'` entries (previously logged with no visible floating indicator) render "Miss" in `CombatPage.tsx`'s floating-number blocks (`text-slate-300`, distinct from the amber/rose damage-number color) alongside the existing "-N" damage numbers, reusing the same `floatingNumbers`/`playerFloatingNumbers` derivation and `AnimatePresence` blocks (both desktop and mobile layouts).
  - **Min/max hit range**: attack rolls around the stored stat value (`rollDamageInRange`/`damageRangeFromMidpoint` in `combatResolver.ts`) — min = mid×`DAMAGE_ROLL_MIN_RATIO`, max = mid×`DAMAGE_ROLL_MAX_RATIO`. Narrowed 2026-08-11 from 0.5/1.5 (±50%, a 3x ratio) to **0.9/1.1** (a 1.222x ratio) — the original ±50% spread was only validated against the level-1 Iron Ring's tiny-integer rounding (min 1/max 3 at mid 2), but the fuller `reference/conquer-items/bows.md`/`rings.md` tables show real Bows running a much tighter ~1.2-1.33x ratio at nearly every level; 0.9/1.1 was picked to match Bows specifically since Hunter/Bow is the only playable class+weapon today (Rings run wider in the real data, ~1.8-2.5x through most levels, only converging to 1.5x at the top few tiers — a known, accepted under-fit since this is one shared constant across every `physical_attack`/`magic_attack` roll). Mirrored in `resolve-combat` (rolled fresh per attack in the simulation loop). Gear tooltips show `min–max` for `physical_attack`/`magic_attack` (via `formatBaseStats`, same constants — so tooltip and combat rolls always match); `StatsPanel.tsx`'s aggregate rows stay flat totals (combining two stats before ranging would mislead).
- **Level-diff color + EXP scaling**: `getLevelDiffColor`/`expMultiplierForLevelDiff` in `combatResolver.ts`. `diff = characterLevel - monsterLevel`. White (-2..2, EXP×1), Green (≥3, EXP×0.5), Red (-3/-4, EXP×1.5), Black (≤-5, EXP×2). Applies identically live and offline. "Black" renders as the darkest still-legible shade (not literal black) on this dark theme.
- **Level-gap Defense debuff**: two separate, asymmetric per-color multiplier tables. **Outgoing** (player hits monster) `MONSTER_DEFENSE_MULTIPLIER_BY_COLOR`: Green ×0.5, White ×0.75, Red/Black ×1 (unaffected). **Incoming** (monster hits player) `PLAYER_DEFENSE_MULTIPLIER_BY_COLOR`: Red ×0.5, Black ×0.1, White/Green ×1 (unaffected) — steepened 2026-08-07 (was Red ×0.75/Black ×0.5, "not a significant enough reduction") so a mismatched fight hits noticeably harder; may need a per-class pass later. `monsterDefense(type, characterLevel)` (mirrored in `resolve-combat/index.ts`, outgoing side only — affects real kills) applies the outgoing multiplier on top of `Math.round(level * 1.5)` base. Incoming side (`effectivePlayerDefense`) is computed inline in `useCombatStore.runTick`, **client-only, not mirrored server-side** (player HP has never been simulated server-side). Only Defense/mitigation is affected — dodge/hit-chance untouched.
- **Monster EXP reward** is formula-derived from monster level, not a stored field (`expRewardForLevel` in `src/game/stats/expCurve.ts`, mirrored in `resolve-combat/index.ts`; `enemy_types.exp_reward` column left in place but unread). `expRewardForLevel(level) = requiredExpForLevel(level) / killsPerLevelForLevel(level)`. Kills-per-level steps up at each promotion tier (1/15/40/70/100/110/120): `PROMOTION_TIER_ANCHORS`/`KILLS_PER_LEVEL_BY_TIER = [200, 350, 650, 1300, 2600, 5200, 10000]` (~10 min/level early → ~8.3 hr/level at tier 7). PLACEHOLDER table. Known minor quirk: EXP reward can dip slightly right at a tier boundary (not fixed, cosmetic).
  - **EXP is on-kill only** — damage-dealt EXP (a flat +50% bonus on top of the full on-kill grant, credited proportionally to damage dealt even on non-killing hits) was **removed in the 2026-11 weapon-curve/enemy-HP rebalance** above, requested by the user alongside the `RESPAWN_GAP_MS` increase to 10s. The live visual damage log line (`useCombatStore.runTick`) shows only the damage number, no EXP value — unchanged.
  - **AFK-cap tier reward**: the bounded elapsed-time window `resolve-combat` will simulate scales with the highest **account-wide Achievements tier** reached on any monster (`account_monster_kills.claimed_tier_index`, `AFK_CAP_MS_BY_ACCOUNT_TIER`) — tier 0 = 2 hours up to tier 6 = 12 hours (2/3/4/6/8/10/12). PLACEHOLDER table. Server-only, no client mirror. (Originally keyed off the per-character Prestige/Kill-Count track; rebased onto the account track since the old one was too trivially reachable.)
  - **Idle/offline EXP rate**: `IDLE_EXP_MULTIPLIER = 0.5` in `resolve-combat/index.ts` — offline catch-up (`mode: 'offline'`) earns EXP at half the live rate, applied once to the window total. Gold unaffected. Server-only.
  - **Death timer**: on player knockout (HP 0), `useCombatStore.reviveAt` locks both sides for `KNOCKOUT_LOCKOUT_MS` (10s placeholder) — `isFighting` stays true, monster HP is NOT reset, fight resumes exactly where it was; player revives to full HP. `start()` clears stale `reviveAt`. `KnockoutHud.tsx` (in `GameShell`'s top HUD strip, alongside `QuiverWarningHud`/`InventoryFullWarningHud`) shows a live "Knocked out — reviving in Ns" countdown while `reviveAt > 0` — added 2026-08-07, the only prior signal was a Combat Log line, easy to miss since the log is collapsible. **Client-only, live combat only** — not mirrored server-side (player HP/death has never been simulated in `resolve-combat`), so offline/idle resolution still doesn't stop a player from idling a monster far above what they could survive live — a known, disclosed gap.
  - Deferred, not built: gating which monsters can be idled/AFK'd (e.g. only after a "Boss" kill — no Boss concept exists); a hard level-gate on selectable monsters.
- **Player HP**: `useCombatStore.currentPlayerHp`/`maxPlayerHp` (from `derivedStats.hp`), continuous across respawns/zone switches (not reset by `start()`/`stop()`/`clear()`, only by knockout). Shown as its own HP bar above the monster card, labeled with character name. Monster attacks on a placeholder flat `attackDamage` per `EnemyTypeDef`, on a fixed cadence (`MONSTER_ATTACK_INTERVAL_MS`, once/sec placeholder) — independent of rare HP/reward scaling. **Known gap**: `offlineProgress.ts` doesn't simulate monster attack-back at all — offline character is effectively invulnerable, a disclosed inconsistency with live combat.
- **Live combat stops on a full Inventory**: `resolveCombat()` takes `mode: 'live' | 'offline'`. Live mode stops simulating the rest of the window the moment a drop can't fit (kill's own gold/EXP already counted) and returns `inventoryFull: true`, never touching `loot_holding`. Offline mode always routes drops to Loot Holding regardless of room (see Loot below). Client: `useCombatStore.stopForInventoryFull()` (sets `isFighting: false`, red `'inventory-full'` log line); `InventoryFullWarningHud.tsx` (in `GameShell`'s top HUD) shows a steady amber "Inventory full" banner reactively, flashing red when a live resolve halts the fight.

## Loot

- Gear drop-rate *template* weighting (which template drops, beyond drop-chance-at-all) is **unresolved — needs deliberate design, not sourced**. Comet/Fallen Star rates are resolved (see `CLAUDE.progression.md`).
- Rare-monster status affects the existing roll rather than being a separate roll.
- **Server-authoritative combat resolution**: `supabase/functions/resolve-combat/index.ts` (Edge Function, invoked via `resolveCombat()` in `src/game/combat/resolveCombat.ts`) is the **sole** grantor of gold/EXP/item-drops/currency-drops for combat. It independently re-derives everything from the DB (never trusts client params): monster from `characters.selected_monster_id`/`current_zone`, combat stats from class+equipment read fresh, monster stats from `enemy_types` (server's own copy of `zoneData.ts` — regenerate if that file changes), elapsed time from `characters.combat_last_resolved_at timestamptz` (server clock, capped per the AFK-tier table above).

## Row Combat (Phase 1, v1.98.0)

A second, **live-only** combat mode alongside the single-target system above — 2 rows of 6 independently-toggled slots per character (12 total), each slot spawning whatever monster is currently selected in the normal Zone & Monster picker at the moment it's toggled on (not a fixed roster, so different slots can host different monster types simultaneously). Row slots attack the player back, client-side only (see below).

- **Ability/passive-only targets — no basic auto-attack (2026-08-17, requested by the user)**: row slots can only take damage from Multi-Shot (below) or future abilities/passives, never from the player's normal 1-attack-per-interval auto-attack. Originally the auto-attack DID target the first enabled+alive slot, same "no manual targeting" shape as single-target combat — removed because with 6 slots to pick off one at a time, plain auto-attack alone cleared Row 1 fast enough that Multi-Shot barely mattered. **Not mutually exclusive with single-target combat** — the normal Zone & Monster fight keeps running in the background exactly as always; toggling a row slot on was never actually wired to pause it (an earlier plan called for that handoff, it never got implemented, and that gap turned out to be exactly the wanted behavior: two independent damage sources, auto-attack on the normal target, abilities on row slots). Stored independently from single-target (`row_combat_last_resolved_at` vs `combat_last_resolved_at`) regardless.
- **No offline/idle path at all** — going AFK (tab hidden/`beforeunload`) must fully stop accrual, unlike single-target's multi-hour AFK cap. Enforced two ways: `RowCombatEngine.tsx` (sibling to `CombatEngine.tsx`, mounted alongside it in `GameShell.tsx`) fires the same visibility/beforeunload-triggered resolve, AND `resolve-row-combat`'s gather RPC applies a hard `ROW_LIVE_LIVENESS_THRESHOLD_MS` (10s) cutoff — any gap past that is zeroed. Matters less than it used to now that nothing accrues passively at all (see below), but still gates how stale a respawn can silently resolve.
- **Schema**: `characters.row_slots jsonb` (12-element array, index 0-5 = Row 1, 6-11 = Row 2; each `{enabled, monster_id, current_hp, max_hp, is_rare, dead_at}`), `row_combat_last_resolved_at`, `row_multi_shot_last_fired_at`, `row1_unlocked`/`row2_unlocked booleans`. All in `supabase/migrations/20260902000000_add_row_combat.sql`, unreachable from a plain client update (no column grant) — every mutation goes through a `security definer` RPC.
- **Reward math — no passive accrual at all, purely discrete actions**: since there's no auto-attack, a plain resolve call (`fireMultiShot: false`) only processes respawns that came due — no reward math runs at all. A `fireMultiShot: true` call applies one **real rolled hit** (not deterministic expected-value math) to every enabled+alive slot — see Multi-Shot's own bullet below. A kill is simply `current_hp` crossing ≤0, so overkill is naturally discarded and damage-dealt EXP (`DAMAGE_EXP_SHARE`) accrues per landed hit regardless of whether it kills. Genuine `Math.random()` calls (drops/quality/currency/pet/rare-flavor/respawn rarity) still happen per real kill, same shape as `resolve-combat` (`PET_DROP_CHANCE`/`JADE_SHARD_DROP_CHANCE` identical) — but **`DROP_CHANCE`/`QUALITY_DROP_CHANCES`/`COMET_DROP_CHANCE`/`FALLEN_STAR_DROP_CHANCE` are deliberately 4x single-target's own values as of v1.99.35 (2026-08-20)**, a Row Combat-specific buff, not a global rebalance — the two functions' copies of just these four constants are intentionally out of sync; everything else must still stay mirrored. (Originally a bounded discrete-event walk also processed per-window auto-attack ticks — removed along with auto-attack itself; `applyHitToSlot` is now only ever called from the Multi-Shot branch.)
  - **Achievement + Gold Donation Event buffs both apply (Gold Donation Event fixed 2026-08-17, reported by the user as missing)**: `resolve_row_combat_gather_state` reads `account_zone_attack_bonus_pct`/`account_zone_drop_bonus_pct` (Achievements, was already correct) and now also `gold_donation_state`/`gold_donation_pools` for the event's active buff (was missing entirely — `resolve-row-combat/index.ts` had no `eventExpMultiplier`/`eventCometMultiplier`/`eventFallenStarMultiplier`/`eventQualityMultiplier` derivation at all before this fix, so a live event buff boosted single-target kills via `resolve-combat` but had zero effect on Multi-Shot). Same query and multiplier-derivation shape as `resolve-combat/index.ts`, added in `supabase/migrations/20260904020000_row_combat_gold_donation_event.sql`.
- **Multi-Shot**: Hunter-only, player-pressed (not automatic), fixed `MULTI_SHOT_COOLDOWN_MS` (10s placeholder, **aligned with `ROW_RESPAWN_MS`**, also 10s as of 2026-08-17 — was 15s, lowered to match) enforced server-side against `row_multi_shot_last_fired_at` — never trusts the client's own cooldown display. No mana/resource cost. No mechanical hook into the old, inert `promotion_tiers.skills_unlocked` "Multi-Shot" flavor text (Falcon Hunter tier 40, still unobtainable — see Accounts & Classes' Class Promotion section) — deliberately granted to all Hunters via a plain `class === 'hunter'` check instead, since gating it behind an unbuildable-until-Mining-ships promotion would block the whole mechanic.
  - **Requires a living target (2026-08-17, requested by the user)**: firing with every enabled slot empty/dead does NOT consume the cooldown (`multiShotNoTarget: true` in the response) — checked after `processRespawnsUpTo(now)`, so a slot whose respawn timer just elapsed still counts. Client mirrors this as a button-disable check (`useRowCombatStore`'s `slots.some(s => s.enabled && s.currentHp > 0)`) purely so the button doesn't invite a press the server would reject anyway — the server check is the real authority.
  - **Real rolled damage, 50% per target (2026-08-17, requested by the user)**: unlike single-target combat's deterministic expected-value reward math, Multi-Shot is a discrete, single, player-pressed action — closer to World Boss's "one real, immediately server-resolved attempt" than to a continuous per-window formula — so it rolls real damage per target: `resolvePhysicalDamage(rollDamageInRange(attackMidpoint), monsterDefense(...))` (same ±10% `DAMAGE_ROLL_MIN_RATIO`/`MAX_RATIO` variance every other attack in this game uses, newly added to `resolve-row-combat/index.ts` since single-target's own server function never needed a roll function), then **halved** (`MULTI_SHOT_DAMAGE_MULTIPLIER = 0.5`, applied to the final post-defense number) since it hits every target at once. Hit chance is also a real roll now (`Math.random() >= hitChance` → miss), not folded into an expected-value fraction. Real RNG here doesn't reintroduce the divergence problem the 2026-08-11 single-target rewrite fixed — that was about the *same* window being simulated twice (client tick vs. server), not about randomness existing; a discrete one-shot action only ever gets rolled once, server-side.
  - **Floating damage numbers**: the server returns `multiShotHits: {slotIndex, hit, damage}[]` in its response; `resolveRowCombat.ts` feeds this to `useRowCombatStore`'s `applyMultiShotHits`, and `RowCombatPanel.tsx`'s `RowSlotTile` renders a floating "-N"/"Miss" per hit slot (same `AnimatePresence`/`motion` visual language as `CombatPage.tsx`'s own floating numbers) — shown only once the server response actually arrives, never predicted client-side, since the damage is real RNG the client doesn't know in advance.
- **Monster attack-back is client-only**, mirroring single-target's own existing, documented gap ("player HP has never been simulated server-side") rather than introducing server-side player-HP simulation across up to 12 independent timers. Player HP/knockout is one shared pool across both modes — `useCombatStore.ts` gained `isKnockedOutAt(nowMs)`/`applyIncomingDamage(damage, nowMs, sourceName)` specifically so `useRowCombatStore`'s tick loop mutates the same HP state single-target's `runTick` does, instead of forking a second pool.
- **Client**: `useRowCombatStore.ts` (12-slot state; its tick loop only drives attack-back and the respawn countdown now — no auto-attack block), `resolveRowCombat.ts` (client wrapper, same "server response reconciles local state" convention as `resolveCombat.ts`), `RowCombatEngine.tsx`. Hydrated from `characters.row1_unlocked/row2_unlocked/row_slots` in `useCharacterRecordStore.ts`'s `loadCharacterRecord` (same place `useCombatStore.clear()` already resets on character switch — row state gets the same reset, to avoid the same "zombie fight" bug class).
- **RPCs**: `resolve_row_combat_gather_state`/`resolve_row_combat_apply_results`/`resolve_row_combat_release_claim` (service-role only, mirror `resolve_combat_*`'s exact-CAS claim/release shape), `toggle_row_slot` (security definer, ownership-checked, reads `selected_monster_id` server-side — never trusts a client-passed monster id; **client must always call `resolveRowCombat()` before toggling**, so pending time settles against pre-toggle slot state), `claim_row_unlock` (security definer, reuses the *existing* per-character Kill Count ladder — Row 1 at claimed tier 2/250 kills, Row 2 at tier 4/1000 kills, both placeholder/tunable — rather than a new achievement track), `clear_row_slots` (security definer, bulk-disables all 12 slots in one call — `supabase/migrations/20260903000000_clear_row_slots_on_target_switch.sql`).
- **Rows clear whenever the player switches which enemy they're fighting (2026-08-17, requested by the user)**: `CombatPage.tsx`'s `clearRowSlotsForCharacter` (optimistic local `applyServerSlots([])` + fire-and-forget `clear_row_slots` RPC) is called from `handleFight` — only on a genuine switch, guarded by `typeId !== monsterTypeId` so resuming the same monster (Stop → Resume) doesn't wipe active rows — and unconditionally from `handleSelectZone` (switching zones already clears single-target combat too). Otherwise Row Combat would keep fighting whatever monster type got locked into each slot at its own toggle-on time even after the player moved their primary target elsewhere.
- **UI**: `RowCombatPanel.tsx`, replacing `CombatPage.tsx`'s old 4-slot `MultiTargetSlots` placeholder (which was scaffolding for this exact feature). ## Mining (v1.102.0-1.103.0, 2026-08-22)

A second idle-combat mode alongside Hunting, reached via the Idling tab's Hunting/Mining/Events switcher (see `CLAUDE.server-events.md`). Mines stationary nodes with a dedicated Pickaxe (not equipped gear) for Ore (sellable) and a chance at Gems. **Mutually exclusive with Hunting, confirmed by the user** — the two can never both accrue progress, live or offline, *for the same character*; starting one stops the other (`CombatPage.tsx`'s `handleFight` / `MiningModePanel.tsx`'s `stopHuntingIfActive`, each calling the other mode's `stop()`, which the respective Engine's own store-subscription picks up to fire a final resolve). `characters.last_active_idle_mode` (`'hunting'|'mining'`, session/cosmetic column, same client-writable treatment as `selected_monster_id`) records which one to resume/offline-catch-up on login — set by `useIdleModeStore`, read once by `GameShell.tsx`'s load effect and its resume heartbeat.
- **Hunting Slot exclusivity, account-wide (2026-10-23, reported by the user)**: unlike the per-character exclusivity above, only **one character per account** may hold a live Hunting session at all — Mining has no such limit, several characters may mine in parallel. Without this, offline/AFK catch-up is resolved independently per character (each character's own `combat_last_resolved_at`), so an account with several characters all parked on Hunting could claim the same real-world elapsed away-time once per character just by visiting each on login. `players.hunting_character_id` (migration `20261023000000_hunting_slot_exclusivity.sql`) is the single source of truth for who holds the slot. `claim_hunting_slot(p_character_id)` — called from `handleFight` whenever Hunting is entered/resumed — is **auto-takeover, no confirmation** (confirmed by the user): always succeeds for the caller's own character, silently clearing the displaced character's `selected_monster_id` server-side and flipping it to Mining mode. That alone is what stops the displaced character's future `resolve-combat` calls (its existing "no monster selected → no-op" early return, unrelated to this fix) — **no `resolve-combat`/Deno change was needed**. `release_hunting_slot(p_character_id)` frees the slot when a character that holds it voluntarily switches itself to Mining (`MiningModePanel.tsx`'s `handleMine`). `HuntingTakeoverToast.tsx`/`useHuntingTakeoverToastStore.ts` (mirrors `PetToast`'s exact shape) shows "took over Hunting from X" using the claim RPC's own `previous_hunter_name` response field. Deleting a character auto-frees the slot (`on delete set null`).
- **Bug, fixed 2026-08-22 (reported by the user — switching Hunting↔Mining replayed the whole away period as a catch-up in the mode just left, in both directions)**: `CombatEngine.tsx`/`MiningEngine.tsx` each subscribe to their own store to fire a "final resolve" the instant `isFighting`/`isMining` flips to `false` (relied on by `stopHuntingIfActive`/`handleFight`'s own comments as sufficient to close out the window on a mode switch). That subscription callback reused the same `resolve()` helper the periodic 4s tick uses, which is itself guarded on `isFighting`/`isMining` being true — but by the time the callback runs, the flag has *already* flipped to `false`, so the guard silently no-op'd every stop-triggered resolve. `combat_last_resolved_at`/`mining_last_resolved_at` never got stamped to "now" on a switch, staying stale at whatever the last periodic tick had set — so resuming the left-behind mode later computed `elapsed = now - last_resolved_at` across the *entire* time spent in the other mode too, and granted a full catch-up as if this mode had been running the whole time. Fixed by having each stop-transition subscription call `resolveCombat`/`resolveMining` directly instead of through the guarded `resolve()` helper.
  - **Second, deeper cause of the same symptom, fixed 2026-09-30 (reported by the user — still saw a full inventory of Ore right after switching from Hunting into Mining, even after the fix above)**: the fix above only closes out the window for the mode being *left*. It does nothing for the mode being *entered* — that mode's own `last_resolved_at` sits completely frozen the entire time it was inactive (nothing ever advances it while its own `isFighting`/`isMining` is false), so the moment it resumes, the very first resolve (the periodic 4s tick, now that the mode is active again) computes `elapsed = now - last_resolved_at` across however long it's been since that mode was last genuinely active — which, after enough Hunting↔Mining switching, could be capped-out stale from hours or days earlier, not just the length of the one session just left. `MINING_AFK_CAP_MS`/combat's own AFK cap bounded it, but still let a full multi-hour catch-up fire on every single switch into an idle mode, indistinguishable from a genuine offline return. Fixed with two new dedicated RPCs, `touch_combat_last_resolved_at`/`touch_mining_last_resolved_at` (`20260930110000_touch_last_resolved_on_mode_switch.sql`, security definer, ownership-checked, pure `set … = now()` with no reward math and no CAS claim) — `handleFight`/`stopHuntingIfActive` now call the *entering* mode's touch RPC right alongside stopping the other one, resetting its pointer to "now" with zero grant before the new session's first resolve can see a stale gap. Deliberately **not** wired into `GameShell.tsx`'s login resume block — that path calls `useCombatStore`/`useMiningStore`'s `start()` directly on the store, bypassing `handleFight`/`handleMine` entirely, so the one place a large elapsed gap genuinely *should* be honored (the login-time offline-progress check) is untouched by this change.
- **Bug, fixed 2026-08-22 (reported by the user — real Hunting knockouts while they were only mining)**: `usePersistGameState.ts`'s debounced autosave never subscribed to `useMineStore`/`useIdleModeStore` — `selected_mine_id`/`last_active_idle_mode` were correctly hydrated on load and correctly included in `saveNow`'s payload, but nothing actually *triggered* a save when either changed, since Mining grants no gold/EXP and touches no zone/equipment store (the only things that DO reschedule a save).
